Mission

Engineering should remove complexity

I believe engineering should remove complexity, automate repetitive work and let teams focus on innovation instead of operations.

Over the last fifteen years I've worked across enterprise infrastructure, cloud architecture, DevOps, Kubernetes and AI-driven automation — helping organisations build platforms that are secure, reliable and scalable by default rather than by escalation.

The work I care most about sits at the boundary between architecture and delivery: making the right thing the easy thing for the engineers who have to live with the system.

  • Secure by default

    Least privilege and isolation designed in, not bolted on after review.

  • Automated over documented

    A runbook is a bug report against your automation.

  • Observable

    If you can't see it in production, you don't operate it — you hope.

  • Boring where it counts

    Novelty belongs in the product, not in the platform underneath it.
